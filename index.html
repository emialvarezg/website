I'm a Ph.D. student at Instituto de Matemáticas, UNAM, in Mexico City. My advisor is Gerónimo Uribe Bravo. Previously, I obtained my M.Sc. in Mathematics at Instituto de Matemáticas and my B.Sc. in Applied Mathematics at Facultad de Ciencias, UNAM, both under the supervision of Saraí Hernández-Torres. I'm interested in discrete probability models and their scaling limits.
